# Linked Billing Beta Terms

Effective 2026-11-10

These Linked Billing Beta Terms (“Linked Billing Terms”) govern Customer’s access to and use of Linked Billing. They are entered into between Anthropic and the entity that accepts them (“Customer”) and take effect on the date Customer first accepts them (the “Effective Date”).

Linked Billing. Anthropic may offer functionality that lets Customer, with authorization from a User that has its own account with Anthropic, make requests to the Services for that User and have Anthropic bill that User directly for the resulting usage (“Linked Billing”). These Linked Billing Terms apply to Customer’s use of Linked Billing.

Acceptance. Customer accepts these Linked Billing Terms by clicking “I accept” (or a similar button or checkbox) presented with them, or by enabling or using Linked Billing. The individual accepting on Customer’s behalf represents that they have the authority to bind Customer to these Linked Billing Terms. If Customer does not agree to these Linked Billing Terms, Customer may not enable or use Linked Billing.

Relationship to the Agreement. These Linked Billing Terms supplement the agreement between Customer and Anthropic that governs Customer’s access to and use of the Services, which is Anthropic’s Commercial Terms of Service or, if Customer and Anthropic have entered into a separate written agreement governing Customer’s use of the Services, that agreement, in each case together with any order forms and other terms incorporated into it (the “Agreement”).

Order of Precedence. If these Linked Billing Terms conflict with the Agreement, these Linked Billing Terms control, but only with respect to Linked Billing. If Customer and Anthropic have signed an order form or amendment that includes terms for Linked Billing, those signed terms control over these Linked Billing Terms.

Defined Terms. Capitalized terms used but not defined in these Linked Billing Terms have the meanings given in the Agreement. References to “Sections” are to sections of these Linked Billing Terms unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Eligibility; First-Party Accounts

Customer may use Linked Billing only if Anthropic has enabled it for Customer’s account. Linked Billing is available only where both Customer and the User use a First-Party Account. “First-Party Account” means an account for Anthropic’s API that Anthropic provides directly, and excludes any account provided, billed, or accessed through a Marketplace Platform and any Claude.ai, Claude for Work, or other subscription or plan account. “Marketplace Platform” means a third-party cloud platform or marketplace through which Anthropic’s models or services are made available. Anthropic may expand the types of accounts eligible for Linked Billing by Notice to Customer or updating its online documentation.

## 2. Linking

2.1 Links. A User may authorize Linked Billing by having an administrator of its First-Party Account complete Anthropic’s authorization process for Customer’s application (each such authorization, a “Link,” and each such User, a “Linked Customer”). Customer must:

(a) start each Link only through Anthropic’s authorization process, and not request, collect, or store any User’s Anthropic password, session credentials, or API keys in connection with Linked Billing;

(b) use reasonable measures to protect the credentials and tokens it receives for Linked Billing, and promptly give Anthropic Notice if it believes any of them, or any Link, has been compromised or misused;

(c) establish Links only with Users that are Customer’s own direct customers, and not with any person that accesses Customer’s products through another reseller or intermediary; and

(d) accurately identify Customer and its application in the authorization process, without impersonating Anthropic or any other person and not misrepresent, obscure, or interfere with what the authorization process presents to the User, including that Anthropic will bill Linked Customers for Linked Usage.

2.2 Status of Linked Customers. A Linked Customer remains Customer’s User under the Agreement. Anthropic’s billing relationship with a Linked Customer is governed by the Linked Customer’s own agreement with Anthropic. Customer is not a party to, and has no rights under, that agreement.

## 3. Linked Usage; Attribution

3.1 Designating Linked Usage. “Linked Usage” means requests to the Services that Customer makes for a Linked Customer and designates, in the manner Anthropic specifies, to be billed to that Linked Customer. To designate Linked Usage, Customer must use Anthropic’s user profiles API functionality or any successor functionality Anthropic designates (“User Profiles”). In establishing a User Profile for each Linked Customer, Customer must:

(a) designate as Linked Usage only requests actually made for that Linked Customer’s use of Customer’s products at Linked Customer’s direction, while its Link is active, and only for features of the Services that Anthropic supports for Linked Billing;

(b) not use one Linked Customer’s Link for any other person’s requests, including Customer’s own; and

(c) promptly correct any inaccurate designation and give Anthropic Notice of any error that materially affects the amounts billed to a Linked Customer.

3.2 Model Selection. Customer may not select models or options for the purpose of increasing a Linked Customer’s usage and must otherwise follow Linked Customer’s instruction regarding model routing within Customer’s products.

## 4. Restrictions

4.1 Permitted Use. Customer may use Linked Billing only to power products and services that Customer makes available to Linked Customers. Unless authorized by Anthropic, Customer may not, and must ensure its Users do not, use Linked Billing or a Link to:

(a) operate a gateway, router, proxy, or similar service that relays access to the Services without materially transforming Inputs or Outputs;

(b) access, power, or operate Claude Code, Claude.ai, Claude for Work, or any other application, client, agent, or interface Anthropic makes available; or

(c) provide general-purpose access to the Services or to a Linked Customer’s account, including for tools other than the application the Linked Customer authorized, or pool, transfer, resell, or share a Link or a Linked Customer’s pricing, commitment, or credits.

4.2 No Markup. Customer may charge its own fees for its products, but may not separately charge a Linked Customer for, or mark up, Linked Usage that Anthropic bills to that Linked Customer.

## 5. Fees

Anthropic will bill Linked Usage to the Linked Customer at the rates that apply to the Linked Customer, and those Fees only count toward any spend commitment of the Linked Customer. Customer is not responsible for Fees for Linked Usage, except as stated in this Section 5. Customer remains responsible for Fees for all other usage of its account, including any request it does not designate as Linked Usage. If Customer improperly designates usage as Linked Usage, as determined solely by Anthropic, Anthropic may credit the affected Linked Customer, and Customer must pay Anthropic the Fees for that usage at the rates that apply to Customer. Anthropic may report Linked Usage to Customer at list price and will not disclose a Linked Customer’s rates to Customer.

## 6. Spend Limits; Disconnection

A Linked Customer may set a limit on its monthly spend for Customer’s application through Anthropic’s interfaces (“Spend Limit”) and may end its Link at any time. Customer may also end a Link at any time. Anthropic may refuse requests that would exceed a Spend Limit or that are made after a Link has ended.

## 7. Data

7.1 Customer Content. A Link does not change how Customer Content is handled. Inputs and Outputs from Linked Usage are User’s customer content, and Anthropic will process them under the Agreement, including the DPA, in accordance with the configuration of Customer’s account (including any retention settings), not the Linked Customer’s. Customer is responsible for telling Linked Customers how their data is handled in Customer’s products, including as stated in this Section 7.1.

7.2 Linked Customer Information. Anthropic may share with Customer the name, email address, organization name, and similar information of a Linked Customer (“Linked Customer Information”). Customer may use Linked Customer Information only to sign the Linked Customer in, to establish and maintain the Link, and to provide and support Customer’s products for that Linked Customer. Customer is independently responsible for its processing of Linked Customer Information and must process it lawfully, limit its collection to what is reasonably necessary, provide all notices and obtain all consents required by applicable law, and delete it when no longer needed for these purposes. Linked Customer Information is not Customer Content.

7.3 Link Information. Anthropic may use the existence of a Link, the identity of the Linked Customer, and data regarding Linked Usage (excluding Inputs and Outputs) to provide, bill for, and report on the Services, to enforce the Agreement, these Linked Billing Terms, and the Usage Policy, and to communicate with the Linked Customer. Anthropic may show a Linked Customer that Customer is linked to its account and its usage and spend through Customer’s products. This information is not Customer’s Confidential Information.

## 8. Customer’s Responsibilities

Customer’s obligations regarding its Users under the Agreement apply to Linked Customers and Linked Usage. Customer is responsible for its products and for supporting Linked Customers’ use of them. Anthropic may suspend or end a Link or User Profile if Anthropic reasonably believes Customer or the Linked Customer is using it in violation of the Agreement, these Linked Billing Terms, or the Linked Customer’s agreement with Anthropic.

## 9. Names and Marks

Customer may use the names and marks that Anthropic provides for Linked Billing in accordance with any guidelines Anthropic provides, solely to enable Users to establish and manage Links. Customer may not state or imply that Anthropic sponsors or endorses Customer or its products. Customer permits Anthropic to display Customer’s name and logo in the Linked Billing authorization flow, in Linked Customers’ account interfaces, as well as on the Claude Marketplace as providing a Linked Billing-authorized solution, regardless of any publicity restriction or opt-out under the Agreement. In connection with such use, Customer grants Anthropic a worldwide, limited-term, non-exclusive, and royalty-free license during the Term to use this content to facilitate Anthropic’s provision of Linked Billing. Customer must have the necessary rights to use (and grant Anthropic the rights to use) this content for the purposes of providing Linked Billing.

## 10. Beta Status; Changes; Effect of Ending

10.1 Beta Status; Changes. Linked Billing is a Beta Service (as defined in the Service Specific Terms) until Anthropic states otherwise. Anthropic may suspend, modify, terminate, or discontinue Linked Billing or Customer’s access to it, and will use reasonable efforts to give Customer Notice in advance. Customer may stop using Linked Billing at any time. Anthropic may update these Linked Billing Terms in the same manner as it may update the Agreement.

10.2 Effect of Ending. When Customer’s access to Linked Billing ends, all of its Links end, and Customer must stop designating Linked Usage and tell affected Linked Customers. Sections 5 (Fees), 7 (Data), and 8 (Customer’s Responsibilities) survive for usage that occurred before then.
