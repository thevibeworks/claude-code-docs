# Respond to an Enterprise domain claim on your Claude account

If your organization has set up Claude Enterprise, they may claim your existing personal Claude account (Free, Pro, Max, or Ultra). This article explains what to expect and what your options are.

**Note:** If your admin hasn't claimed your domain yet and you're exploring your options, see **[Move your personal Claude account to a Team or Enterprise organization](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9267400-move-your-personal-claude-account-to-a-team-or-enterprise-organization)**.

## What it means when your domain is claimed

Your organization’s admin has initiated a process called domain claiming. This means all personal Claude accounts tied to your company’s email domain (for example, *@yourcompany.com*) will close. Before a deadline, you choose whether to bring what you can into your organization’s Enterprise workspace.

You’ll receive an email and an in-product notification letting you know that your account has been claimed, along with a deadline to choose what happens to your data. This deadline is at least 30 days from the date your admin initiates the claim.

The notification is a banner at the top of Claude, on the web and in Claude Desktop, titled "Choose what happens to your personal data." Click "See options" to make a choice. The banner appears in your personal account, and in your organization if you’ve already joined.

## Your two options

**Note:** If your organization has turned on HIPAA readiness or customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK), the **Bring what you can** option isn't available. Your only option is to start fresh. **[Export your data](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9450526-export-your-claude-data)** before the deadline if you want a copy. The export doesn’t include the original files you uploaded. Your subscription is still handled as described below. Learn more about **[HIPAA-ready Enterprise plans](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13296973)** and **[customer-managed encryption keys](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15505325)**.

### Bring what you can

Bring your existing data into your organization's Enterprise workspace. Some things don't move. Most importantly, custom skills, Claude Docs, Cowork tasks in the cloud, and your connected apps are left in your personal account. To bring your data, you need a seat in your organization. Ask your admin for one. Before you confirm, you’ll see the list of what will and won’t move from your account. Save what won’t move first (see **[Before you migrate](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9267400-move-your-personal-claude-account-to-a-team-or-enterprise-organization#h_c1ec8a383d)**). For the full list of what does and doesn’t move, see **[What moves when you migrate](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9267400-move-your-personal-claude-account-to-a-team-or-enterprise-organization#h_4e63ef6e87)**.

After the move, your personal account closes and will be deleted, along with anything that didn’t move, per our data retention practices. **This can’t be undone.** If you subscribed through the Apple App Store, your personal account closes a few weeks after you move, then it's deleted, per our data retention practices. What moved stays in your organization’s workspace. Learn more about **[how long Anthropic stores your data](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data)**.

### Start fresh

Your existing data won’t move to your organization’s Enterprise workspace. Your personal account closes, and its data will be deleted, per our **[data retention practices](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data)**.

If you want a copy, you can get one in two ways:

- Before you choose "Start fresh," sign in to your personal account, go to **[Settings > Privacy](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls)**, export your data, and download the file.

- After you choose "Start fresh," pick "Download your data." We’ll email you a link after your personal account closes. The link expires after 24 hours and may work only once, so download the file right away. Claude for Teachers and FERPA-covered accounts don’t get a download link. Export and download your data before you choose **Start fresh**. For Claude for Teachers, go to **[Organization settings > Data and privacy](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/data-privacy-controls)**. For FERPA-covered accounts, go to **[Settings > Privacy](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls)**. If you can’t export, **[contact Support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support#h_d6fc679aa4)** before you choose **Start fresh**.

Note that exported data can’t be imported into your organization’s workspace, and it’s for your personal records only. Neither copy includes the original files you uploaded.

## What happens if you don’t choose

**If you don’t make a choice before the deadline, your personal account closes and will be deleted, along with its data**, per our **[data retention practices](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data).** You’ll get an email with a link to download your data. The download doesn’t include the original files you uploaded.

Download the file right away: the link expires after 24 hours and may only work only. It opens only when you’re signed in to Claude with the same email address. If it has expired or won’t open, **[contact Support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support#h_d6fc679aa4)** (an Owner of your organization may need to reach out for you). Claude for Teachers and FERPA-covered accounts don’t get a download link. Export and download your data before the deadline.

For Claude for Teachers, go to **[Organization settings > Data and privacy](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/data-privacy-controls)**. For FERPA-covered accounts, go to **[Settings > Privacy](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls)**. If you can’t export, **[contact Support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support#h_d6fc679aa4)** before the deadline. To use Claude in your organization, sign in with single sign-on (SSO). If you can't sign in or don’t see your organization after you sign in, ask your admin to add you. The download link won’t open until you can sign in.

Sign in before the deadline to make your choice.

## What happens to your paid subscription

If you bought a Pro, Max, or Ultra subscription directly from us, it will be automatically canceled when you choose an option or when the deadline passes. You’ll receive a prorated refund for any unused time. If you subscribed through Google Play, we ask Google Play to cancel your subscription and refund your unused time. Check Google Play, and cancel there if your plan is still active. See **[How do refunds work for migrated accounts?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9267400-move-your-personal-claude-account-to-a-team-or-enterprise-organization#h_c0aa7afb70)**

**Apple App Store subscriptions:** If you purchased your Pro, Max, or Ultra plan through the Apple App Store, we can’t cancel or refund it directly. You’ll need to cancel it yourself through your Apple ID settings:

1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.

2. Tap your name at the top, then tap "Subscriptions."

3. Find your Claude subscription and tap "Cancel Subscription."

If you don’t cancel your App Store subscription, Apple will continue to charge you even though your personal account has been closed.

## How to sign in after migration

After migration, you’ll sign in to Claude using your organization’s single sign-on (SSO) provider. Go to claude.ai and sign in with your work email—you’ll be redirected to your company’s login page. When a move finishes or your personal account closes, you’re signed out on all your devices, so you’ll need to sign in again.

**Can’t sign in?** You may not be set up in your company’s identity provider yet. Contact your IT admin for help.

If you chose "Bring what you can," what moved will be waiting in your organization’s workspace.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I undo a move?

No. Moving is permanent. Once your personal account data is transferred to your organization’s workspace, it can’t be moved back to a personal account. Start fresh can’t be undone either.

### Can I keep my personal account and also join the Enterprise workspace?

No. Once your domain is claimed, personal accounts on that domain can’t coexist with the Enterprise workspace. You’ll need to choose "Bring what you can" or "Start fresh."

### What if I use my personal email, not my work email?

Domain claiming only affects accounts tied to your organization’s verified domain (your work email). If you have a separate Claude account on a personal email address (like Gmail or Yahoo), that account isn’t affected.

### Will my organization be able to see my old conversations?

Yes. Your organization can access what moves, the same way it can access the rest of its data. For example, its Primary Owner can export it. Your organization’s data retention policies apply to it too. For more information, see **[Who owns and manages the data of my team?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9265372-who-owns-and-manages-the-data-of-my-team)**

### What if I’m not in my company’s identity provider?

If you’re not in your organization’s IdP, you won’t be able to sign in to the Enterprise workspace after migration. Contact your IT admin to make sure you’re added before the deadline. Your admin must add you, with a seat, before you can bring your data.

### I have both a personal account and an existing Enterprise account. What happens?

You can choose "Bring what you can." What can move is added to your organization’s workspace. If your personal and organization settings conflict, your organization’s settings apply.
