# Manage Anthropic email updates for your organization members

This article explains how Owners and Primary Owners on Enterprise plans can turn Anthropic email updates on or off for members of their organization.

## What this setting does

When the Email updates from Anthropic setting is on, Anthropic will send members of your organization emails that help them get more from Claude, including new features, tips, and occasional offers. Members will still receive account, security, and billing emails, and can manage their preferences or unsubscribe at any time.

This setting doesn’t affect updates sent to Owners and Primary Owners. Members with a custom role are affected, even if the role includes Owner access. The Email updates from Anthropic setting is on by default.

- The setting applies to email only, and to one organization. If your company has more than one organization, change the setting in each one.

- Members can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email. While this setting is off, members cannot restart these emails on their own.

- Members are not notified when you change this setting.

## What members receive when this setting is off

| **Stops**                                                                                           | **Continues**                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Emails that help members get more from Claude, including new features, tips, and occasional offers  | Transactional notices, such as account, security, billing, and legal emails and sign-in links                                                                                     |
| Invitations an admin sends from Claude to try a product or use credits, such as Claude Code invites | Product notifications, such as invitations to join your organization, a colleague sharing a project, comments on an artifact, seat updates, and answers to requests a member made |
|                                                                                                     | Emails a member asked for or opted into, such as data exports                                                                                                                     |
|                                                                                                     | Webinar and event invitations                                                                                                                                                     |

**Note:** This setting doesn't affect emails to Owners, Primary Owners, and admins about running your organization.

## Who can change this setting

Owners and Primary Owners of Enterprise plans can change this setting. Members with a custom role that includes Owner access can change it too.

The setting isn't available for every Enterprise organization. If you're an Owner and don't see it, contact Support.

**[Learn more about roles and permissions](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9267276-roles-and-permissions)**

## Turn email updates off or on

To turn email updates off or on:

1. Navigate to **[Organization settings > Organization and access](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/organization)**.

2. Go to **Anthropic support and updates**. In some organizations, this section is called **Anthropic updates**.

3. Find **Email updates from Anthropic**.

**To turn email updates off:** Turn the switch off, then select “Turn off” in the dialog.

**To turn email updates back on:** Turn the switch on. There is nothing to confirm. The switch shows as on right away, but the change itself can take longer, as the note below explains.

- The setting saves automatically. There is no Save button or confirmation message, so the switch position shows the current setting.

- If the change doesn’t save, you see "Email updates from Anthropic could not be saved. You can try again."

- Each change is recorded in your organization's audit log and in the Compliance API.

**Note:** Changes to this setting can take some time to take effect. After you turn it off, members may still receive an email during that time.

## Common questions

### Why is this setting already off?

If your organization asked Anthropic to stop emailing its members before this setting existed, it may already be off. You can turn it on or leave it off.

### Can I turn this off for only some members?

No. The setting applies to the whole organization. Individual members can use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

### What if someone belongs to more than one organization?

If any organization they belong to as a member has this setting off, they stop receiving these emails.

### Can I stop product notifications too?

Not with this setting. Product notifications are separate from Anthropic email updates. To ask about turning off some product notifications, contact **[our support team](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support)**.
