# Claude Fable 5.2 launch offers

To mark the launch of Claude Fable 5.2, we’re giving subscribers on eligible paid plans extra Fable usage for a limited time. Plans and seats that include Fable models get one to three Fable resets based on how long they’ve subscribed. Plans and seats that use Fable models with usage credits get Fable models included for one week, plus one Fable reset. This article explains what each plan gets, how a Fable reset works, and how to use one.

These offers are available on Pro plans, Max plans, Ultra plans, standard and premium seats on Team plans, and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans. They aren’t available on the Free plan, standard seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans, self-serve Enterprise plans, or usage-based Enterprise plans.

## What each plan gets

| **Plan**                                                                                                     | **What you get**                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pro plans and standard seats on Team plans                                                                   | All Fable models included in your plan for one week, up to 50% of your weekly usage limits, plus one Fable limit reset |
| Max plans, Ultra plans, premium seats on Team plans, and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans | One to three Fable limit resets, depending on how long you’ve subscribed                                               |

Both offers start when Fable 5.2 launches on November 9, 2026. They cover every Fable model, including Fable 5.2, Fable 5.1, and Fable 5.

Resets go only to accounts with paid billing in place before November 9, 2026 at 10:00 am PT. For Team and Enterprise seats, the organization's billing must have been in place by then. Seat holders must also have joined the organization before then. If your subscription started before then but is still in a free trial, you get your resets once it converts to paid. Self-serve Team trials count as paid.

Resets have no cash value, can't be transferred, and may be removed for fraud, abuse, or violation of our terms.

## How a Fable limit reset works

A Fable limit reset does two things:

- **Weekly limit:** gives you back the usage you’ve spent on Fable models. Usage you’ve spent on other Claude models stays where it is.

- **Five-hour limit, if your account has one:** refills the whole five-hour window, including usage spent on other models.

For example, say you’ve used 80% of your weekly limit: 50% on Fable models and 30% on other models. After a Fable reset, you’re back to 30% of your weekly limit. If your account has a five-hour limit, it’s fully refilled.

A reset doesn’t raise your limits. It restores usage so you can keep working within your existing limits.

Once you use a reset, you can't undo it. If you cancel or downgrade before using a reset, it's no longer available. For more information, see **[What is a limit reset?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17007452)**

**Note:** You can use a reset at any time before it expires. You don’t have to wait until you hit a limit.

Verified Ultra accounts don’t have a five-hour limit, so a reset only affects your weekly limit. Learn more in **[What is a verified Ultra account?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan#h_ff4fcea367)**

## Fable limit resets based on subscription length

If you’re on a Max or Ultra plan, a premium seat on a Team plan, or a premium seat on a legacy seat-based Enterprise plan, the number of Fable resets you get depends on how long you’ve been a paid subscriber:

| **Subscription length** | **Fable resets** |
| ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| Less than six months    | One              |
| Six to 12 months        | Two              |
| More than 12 months     | Three            |

Subscription length is measured from the date of your first paid bill. If you subscribed through an app store, it’s measured from your first app store bill. If you canceled and came back, the time since your first paid bill still counts. On Team and Enterprise plans, subscription length is measured from your organization's first paid bill, so every premium seat in an organization gets the same number of resets.

These resets expire on March 8, 2027 at 11:59 pm PT. Any resets you haven’t used by then lapse. You can see the expiry date next to your resets in **[Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage)**.

## A week of Fable models included in your plan

If you’re on a Pro plan or a standard seat on a Team plan, all Fable models are included in your plan’s weekly usage limits from launch on November 9, 2026 through November 16, 2026 at 11:59 pm PT. During that week, you can use up to 50% of your weekly usage limits on Fable models at no extra cost, the same way plans that include Fable models can. After you use that 50%, or once the week ends, Fable usage is billed to usage credits if you have them turned on.

You also get one Fable limit reset if you meet the billing cutoff above. The reset expires on November 16, 2026 at 11:59 pm PT.

**Note:** Your default model doesn’t change. To use a Fable model, select it from the model picker.

**Important:** Fable models use your usage limits faster than other Claude models. Keep an eye on your usage in **[Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage)**, and use your Fable reset if you run out of Fable usage before the week is over.

When the week ends, Fable models go back to running on usage credits on Pro plans and standard seats on Team plans. Learn more about **[Claude Fable models on your plan](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15424964)**.

## How to use a Fable reset

1. Go to **[Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage)**.

2. Under **Limit resets**, select “Reset for free.”

Your Fable usage is restored right away. You can use a reset at any time before it expires, not only when you’ve reached a limit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a Fable limit reset raise my usage limits?

No, your usage limits stay the same, including the cap of 50% of your weekly usage limits on Fable models. A reset gives you back usage you’ve already spent, so you can use Claude more than you otherwise could before your limits reset next. It restores the Fable usage on your weekly limit and, if your account has a five-hour limit, refills that too.

### Which models does a reset cover?

Every Fable model: Fable 5.2, Fable 5.1, and Fable 5. The same goes for the one week of Fable access on Pro plans and standard seats on Team plans.

### What happens after the week of included Fable models?

Fable models go back to running on usage credits, and they no longer draw from your plan’s weekly usage limits. Any unused portion of the 50% allowance doesn’t carry over, and an unused reset expires at the end of the window.

### Why did I get fewer Fable limit resets than someone else?

The number of resets is based on how long you've been a paying Claude subscriber, measured at launch from your first paid bill (or your organization's on Team and Enterprise plans), even if you paused or switched plans since. Under six months gets one reset, six to 12 months two, and more than 12 months three.

### I canceled and resubscribed. How is my subscription length counted?

From your first paid bill, not from when you came back. A gap in your subscription doesn’t reset the clock.

### Does this apply to Team and Enterprise plans?

Standard seats on Team plans get a week of included Fable models plus one reset. Premium seats on Team plans and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans get one to three Fable resets based on subscription length. Standard seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans, self-serve Enterprise plans, and usage-based Enterprise plans aren’t included.

### Can I use a Fable limit reset on other models?

On your weekly limit, a Fable limit reset only restores usage spent on Fable models. If your account has a five-hour limit, the reset refills all of it, including usage spent on other models.

### Can I use a Fable limit reset on my HIPAA-ready Enterprise plan?

No, Fable limit resets are not available for HIPAA-ready Enterprise plans.
