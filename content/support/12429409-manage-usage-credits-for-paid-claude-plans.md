# Manage usage credits for paid Claude plans

This article explains how usage credits work on your paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, or Ultra), allowing you to continue working with Claude after reaching your plan's usage limits by switching to pay-as-you-go pricing.

## What are usage credits?

Usage credits allow individuals subscribed to paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, and Ultra) to continue using Claude after reaching their included usage limits. Most paid plans have a session limit that resets every five hours and a weekly limit. The Ultra plan has a weekly limit, and verified Ultra accounts don't have a five-hour limit (**[learn more](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan#h_ff4fcea367)**). Max and Ultra plans also have a separate weekly limit for Fable. Instead of being blocked when you reach one of your plan's limits, you can switch to consumption-based pricing at standard API rates and continue your work without interruption.

Usage credits are separate from the monthly API credits included with Max and Ultra plans. Monthly API credits don’t apply to usage credits in Claude, Claude Code, or Claude Cowork. Learn more about **[monthly API credits for Max, Ultra, and Team plans](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17154008)**.

**Note:** You can buy usage credits and set a spend limit in the Claude mobile apps where in-app purchases are available. Auto-reload is only available on the web.

---

## How usage credits work

When you fully use your plan’s included usage limits (any five-hour session limit, your weekly limit, or your Fable weekly limit on Max and Ultra plans) with usage credits enabled:

- Your requests switch to pay-as-you-go pricing on usage credits and keep working.

- You may briefly still see the notification that you’re approaching your usage limit. Once your next request goes through on usage credits, it changes to “Now using credits.” You haven’t been cut off; your work continues.

- Your subsequent usage will be billed at standard API pricing rates.

- Your usage continues until you reach your monthly spend limit, or your balance runs out with auto-reload off.

- Any session limit resets every five hours and your weekly limits reset once a week, as usual.

**Note:** Usage credits are charged separately from your paid Claude subscription and appear as additional charges on your bill.

## Enable usage credits

To enable usage credits on your paid Claude plan:

1. Navigate to[**Settings > Usage**](https://claude.ai/settings/usage) in your Claude account.

2. Locate the **Usage credits** section and click the toggle to enable them.

3. Set up your payment method if not already configured.

4. Configure your spending preferences.

5. Click “Adjust limit” to control costs with a monthly spend limit. You can also select “Set to unlimited” if you prefer no spending restrictions.

6. You’ll then need to purchase credits to cover usage beyond your plan's limits. Click "Buy usage credits," choose the amount you want to purchase, then click "Continue." The expiration date for your purchase is shown before you confirm.

7. You can also enable auto-reload to automatically make a purchase when your balance falls below a threshold you set.

Control your usage credit spending with:

- **Auto-reload settings:** Automatically purchase additional credits when your balance drops below a threshold.

- **Usage alerts:** Receive notifications when approaching your usage credit limits.

## Expiration and refunds

**Expiration.** Generally, usage credits purchased on or after December 7, 2026 expire 12 months after the purchase date. This applies to one-time purchases, usage bundles, and auto-reload purchases. Any unused credits are removed from your balance when they expire. Credits purchased before December 7, 2026 don't expire.

Expiration dates may vary in certain countries. For more information, see the **[Consumer Credit Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-credit-terms)**.

Your expiration dates are shown on the **Usage** page in Settings, and we'll email you before your purchased credits expire.

**Refunds.** Usage credit purchases are non-refundable, including if you cancel your subscription or delete your account. Credits you've already used are never refunded. Exceptions apply where local law requires them, or in cases of fraud. Learn more about **[requesting a refund for a paid Claude plan](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12386328)**.

## Manage your usage credits

### Monitor usage and costs

Track your usage credits through:

- **Usage dashboard:** View real-time consumption in **[Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage)**.

- **Current spending:** Monitor your month-to-date usage credit costs.

- **Usage history:** Review past consumption patterns to optimize future usage.

## Tips for cost-effective usage

To manage usage credit costs while maintaining productivity:

- **Choose efficient models:** Use our most efficient Haiku model, or the most recent Sonnet model for most tasks. These provide excellent performance at lower token costs than Opus models.

- **Use your plan’s included usage first:** If your plan has a session limit, plan intensive work sessions around your five-hour usage windows.

- **Start new conversations:** Begin fresh chats for new topics to minimize the size of your context window.

- **Use projects effectively:** Store frequently referenced documents in project knowledge instead of re-uploading.

- **Set appropriate limits:** Start with conservative spending caps and adjust based on your needs.

## How usage credits work with other features

### Claude Code

Usage credits apply to both Claude conversations and Claude Code terminal usage. Your combined usage across both interfaces counts toward your limits.

### Research mode

When using Research mode, usage credits apply once you exceed your plan's included limits. Research sessions may consume tokens more quickly due to multiple searches and comprehensive analysis.

### Projects and files

Documents stored in project files count toward your context when used in conversations. Usage credits apply to all tokens processed, including project content.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I disable usage credits after enabling them?

Yes, you can disable usage credits at any time through **[Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage)**. Once disabled, you'll only have access to your plan's included usage.

### How quickly do my regular usage limits reset?

Most paid plans include two usage limits: a session limit that resets every five hours, and a weekly limit that resets once a week. Verified Ultra accounts don't have a five-hour limit (**[learn more](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17728952-what-is-the-ultra-plan#h_ff4fcea367)**). Max and Ultra plans also have a separate weekly limit for Fable that resets once a week. Usage credits don’t affect the reset timing of any of these limits.

### Can I keep using Claude after I reach my weekly limit?

Yes. With usage credits enabled, once one of your included limits is fully used (any five-hour session limit or one of your weekly limits), your requests switch to pay-as-you-go pricing on usage credits and keep working. Your usage continues until you reach your monthly spend limit or your usage credit balance runs out with auto-reload off.

### I subscribed to a paid Claude plan on the App or Play store; how can I access usage credits?

If you purchased your paid plan through one of the Claude mobile apps, you can buy usage credits and set a spend limit in the app where in-app purchases are available. Auto-reload is only available on the web.

### Can I track usage credits separately from my included usage?

Yes, the usage dashboard clearly distinguishes between your included plan usage limits and any usage credit consumption.

### Will I get a warning before switching to usage credits?

Yes. You’ll see a notification when you’re approaching your session limit or one of your weekly limits. That notification may still be showing at the moment your usage switches to usage credits, so it can look like you’ve been cut off. If usage credits are enabled and you have funds available, your next request goes through on usage credits and the notification changes to “Now using credits.”

### Do usage credits expire?

Yes. Generally, usage credits purchased on or after December 7, 2026 expire 12 months after purchase. Credits purchased before that date don't expire. **[Expiration dates differ](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-credit-terms)** in certain jurisdictions. You can view expiration dates on the Usage page in Settings, and we'll email you before your purchased credits expire. Learn more in **[Expiration and refunds](#h_3499c80e6a)** above.

### What happens to my usage credits if I cancel my subscription?

Your credits aren't refunded when you cancel. Your balance stays on your account, but usage credits can only be used with an active paid plan.

### What happens to my usage credits if I delete my account?

Any remaining usage credits are forfeited when you delete your account, and they aren't refunded. Use your balance before deleting your account.

### Can I get a refund for usage credits?

Usage credit purchases are non-refundable except where local law requires it, or in cases of fraud. See **[Expiration and refunds](#h_3499c80e6a)** above for details.
