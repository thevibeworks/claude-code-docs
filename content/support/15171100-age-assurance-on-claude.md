# Age assurance on Claude

Claude, our consumer product, is only available to people over 18 years. You’ll need to confirm you’re 18 or over while setting up an account. When we detect signals that you may be under 18, we'll ask you to verify your age before you can continue using Claude.

## How this works

We have safety systems in place to detect if people under 18 may be using Claude and we’ll disable accounts based on indicators of minor activity.

You’ll have the option to verify your age using one of our third-party age verification providers, Yoti or Persona. A link for verification will be provided in the notification email. If you pass the age verification process, your account will be reinstated.

## Accepted age verification methods

There are several ways to confirm you're 18 or over. Available options may vary:

- Facial age estimation: Take a selfie and the provider’s technology estimates your age. No ID document needed to start. You may be asked for an ID if more information is needed.

- ID verification: Take a photo of your ID (such as a passport, driving license, or national ID card) and upload it from your device. Accepted IDs vary by country and provider. If you're verifying with Yoti, see their **[full list of supported documents](https://developers.yoti.com/identity-verification/supported-documents)**.

- Yoti’s Digital ID app: If you already have the Yoti app, share a verified "over 18" attribute from it.

## How we protect your data

Yoti and Persona are independently audited age verification providers for SOC2 compliance. Your selfie, document images, and any biometric information are deleted by the provider after your age is checked. Anthropic doesn't store your ID or image. We keep the result of the check and details about it, which can include the method used.
