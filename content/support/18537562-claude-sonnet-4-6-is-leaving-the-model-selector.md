# Claude Sonnet 4.6 is leaving the model selector

On December 8, 2026, we’re removing Claude Sonnet 4.6 from the model selector in Claude on the Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra plans. We recommend Claude Sonnet 5.5 as a replacement. This article explains what’s changing, where Sonnet 4.6 stays available, and what happens to your existing chats.

This change applies to chat and Claude Cowork on the Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra plans, including in Claude Desktop and Claude Mobile.

## What’s changing and when

Until December 8, 2026, you can still select Sonnet 4.6 in the model selector in Claude.

Starting December 8, Sonnet 4.6 no longer appears in the model selector.

## Where Sonnet 4.6 stays available

This change doesn’t affect Sonnet 4.6 in:

- Claude Code

- Team and Enterprise plans

- Claude Platform (API)

## What happens to your existing chats in Claude

Your Sonnet 4.6 chats stay in your account, and nothing is deleted. Starting December 8, chats begin moving to Sonnet 5.5 or your default model as you continue them. Not every chat, task, or scheduled task will switch automatically, so we recommend choosing Sonnet 5.5 in each one before December 8. You can switch to a different model at any time.

To keep a copy of your chats, you can export your data at any time. Learn more about **[exporting your Claude data](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9450526)**.

## Switch models

You can change the model at any point in a chat. The change applies starting with Claude’s next response.

1. Click the model name next to the send button.

2. Choose a model, or click “More models” to see additional options.

Learn more about **[changing the model, effort, and thinking settings](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8664678)**.
