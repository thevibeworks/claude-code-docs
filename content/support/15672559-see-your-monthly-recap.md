# See your monthly recap

Your monthly recap shows you how you’ve been using Claude—the topics you spent time on, when you tend to reach for it, and where you might try something new. It’s designed to reflect your patterns back without judgment so you can decide what, if anything, you’d like to change.

The monthly recap is available to users with Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra plans on the web, Claude Desktop, and Claude for iOS and Android. Your recap only appears when memory is turned on, and it isn't available on Team or Enterprise plans. It's available in all regions where Claude is offered.

## Open your recap

1. Open Claude on the web or Claude Desktop.

2. Go to **Settings > Wellbeing** and, under **Reflect**, select "View" (or go to **Settings > Reflect** in the older Settings layout).

3. Your recap loads for the past month. Click the “Past month” dropdown at the top and select another option to load your recap for a different range (past 3 months, past 6 months, past year). Each range ends with the last full month. If you had no activity last month, "Past month" shows this month so far.

4. You can click the refresh icon next to the dropdown to regenerate the recap for the current range. Your recap is saved, so you normally see the same recap for a period on all your devices.

On iOS or Android: tap the menu button at the top left, tap your picture or initials at the bottom of the menu to open Settings, then tap "Reflect."

**Note:** This feature is rolling out gradually. If you don’t see it yet, check back later.

## What your recap shows

### Opening summary

A short headline and paragraph describing your month with Claude. It names the actual work and themes from your conversations rather than offering generic praise.

### Your time with Claude

Three headline stats: your **most active day**, your **peak hour**, and your **total conversations**. Below them, a daily activity chart shows how your conversations were spread across the period.

To set quiet hours and breaks, go to **Settings > Wellbeing** (in the older Settings layout, click "Set quiet hours and breaks" below the chart). See **[Set break reminders and quiet hours](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15672868)** for details.

### What you used Claude for

A proportional breakdown of the themes and topics you brought to Claude over the period, each with a short description and percentage.

### Expanding your skills

Under "Where you're working well with Claude," you see short notes on habits that are working for you, each with a check mark. Under "2 things to try" (or "One thing to try") you see one or two cards, each with a suggestion from your activity and a button to act on it, such as "Copy a prompt" or "Connect this tool." Either list can be absent. Select "Learn more" to read about AI fluency.

A recap that was saved in the older layout shows cards under the AI fluency skills (up to four on the web and Claude Desktop, up to three on iOS and Android), until you regenerate it.

Observations drawn from your own conversations, framed around four AI fluency skills:

- **Delegation**: what to hand off, and what to keep

- **Description**: the context you give Claude

- **Discernment**: weighing what comes back

- **Diligence**: how you represent AI use in your work

Each card pairs an observation with a concrete next step you can take, like creating a skill or a project. Click “Learn more” to read more about the framework.

### A moment to reflect

Three questions on iOS and Android. Selecting one opens a new chat with that question.

### Goals

On Pro, Max, and Ultra plans, type a goal, and Claude sets up a scheduled check-in on it with you.

### Decide when Claude is off

On iOS and Android, and in the older Settings layout on the web: a shortcut to your quiet hours and break reminders.

### At the bottom of the page

Two thumbs to rate the recap, the sentence on memory, and "Delete stored reflections."

## What’s included in your recap

Claude builds your recap from your recent chats, your Claude Code sessions, and Cowork tasks in the cloud. A line on the page says which of these your recap used. For example, "Based on your Claude chats, cloud Claude Code sessions, and cloud Cowork tasks, not sessions or tasks that run only on your computer."

Your recap only appears when memory is on. If you turn memory off, the recap is hidden. Turning memory off hides your stored recaps, and doesn't delete them. To remove them, use "Delete stored reflections" on the Reflect page, with memory on or off.

Your chats on all your devices are included.

### Content from connected services

When Claude pulls content from a connected service like Gmail or Google Drive into a chat, your recap doesn't incorporate the raw email or file. It may incorporate anything Claude wrote in the chat about it. For example, if you ask Claude in a chat to summarize your inbox, the emails themselves aren't pulled into your recap, but the summary Claude wrote is.

### Conversations and sessions that are left out

Your recap is designed to leave out some conversations and sessions, including:

- Incognito chats

- Conversations that use a health integration, such as Apple Health or Health Connect

- Conversations that used your linked accounts in Personal Finance, including their titles

- Claude Code sessions and Cowork tasks that run only on your computer

- Cloud sessions where we can tell Claude used a tool on your computer, phone or browser

- Sessions that use a health integration or your linked accounts in Personal Finance.

- We still record the time you have Claude open during them.

Learn more about **[memory](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11817273)**, **[incognito chats](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12260368)**, and health features on **[iOS](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11869619-use-claude-with-ios-apps#h_21f4b10d6d)** and **[Android](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11869629-use-claude-with-android-apps#h_62b72ef926)**.

## How your recap handles sensitive topics

People bring all kinds of things to Claude, and some conversations touch on difficult or personal subjects. These are handled with care in your recap:

- Recaps are designed not to lead with sensitive or distress-related topics, or to itemize them with counts or percentages.

- Where appropriate, support resources appear alongside your recap.

## Delete your stored recaps

Your recaps are stored until you delete them, a chat or session they used is deleted, or you delete your account. A new recap can also replace or remove the one for the same months. In some cases, a recap can stay stored for longer. Recaps of earlier months can be found in your data export. To delete them, select "Delete stored reflections," or delete an underlying chat or session, or your account.

To remove them, open your recap, and select "Delete stored reflections," the last thing on the page, then select "Delete." This removes the stored recaps of the account you're using, for all periods, on all your devices, and can't be undone. In some cases, a recap can stay stored for longer. Learn more about **[our data retention practices](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data)**.

Your chats aren't deleted. Ratings and notes that you sent as feedback, and the record of the time you have Claude open, stay. Your goals also stay. With memory on, a new recap may be generated the next time you open the page.

## Turn your recap off

Your recap is available by default for eligible accounts where memory is on.

You can disable it by turning **Generate memory from chats** off in **Settings > Your Claude** (or Settings > Memory in the older Settings layout). There is no dedicated toggle for the recap, so if the memory setting is on, the recap will also be enabled. Even with memory enabled, your recap is only generated when you open it (**Settings > Wellbeing**, then "View" under **Reflect**, or the **Settings > Reflect** page in the older Settings layout); if you don't open it, Claude won't create a recap for you.

Turning memory off hides your stored recaps, and doesn't delete them. To remove them, use "Delete stored reflections" on the Reflect page, with memory on or off.

Turning the recap off doesn't affect your chat history or any break reminders or quiet hours you've already set in **Settings > Wellbeing** (or **Settings > Time and focus** in the older Settings layout). We still record the time you have Claude open.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does viewing my recap use my plan’s usage?

Viewing your recap doesn’t count toward your usage limits. If you start a conversation from a suggestion on the page, that conversation counts toward your usage like any other chat.

### Why don’t I see Reflect in Settings?

Check that you're on a Free, Pro, Max, or Ultra plan. The recap isn't available on Team or Enterprise plans.
​
On iOS and Android, update the app to its newest version. On iPhone and iPad, it needs iOS 26 or later for now. The monthly recap isn't on every account yet, so accounts on the same plan may not all see it. On the web and Claude Desktop, in the updated Settings layout, your recap is under **Settings > Wellbeing**.

### Why does a percentage or stat look off?

Claude generates your recap's summary and topic breakdown from your chats and your sessions and tasks in the cloud, so occasional inaccuracies can occur in the proportions or the narrative summary. Use the thumbs feedback on the page to let us know if something looks wrong. If you use more than one Claude account, each account’s recap covers that account only.

### Why is some of my Claude Code or Cowork work missing?

Your recap reads sessions and tasks that run in the cloud. If you ran many, only the newest are read, so older ones can be missing. For a long session, only the start is read, and in a few cases a long session is left out. A session can also be missing if it couldn't be read when your recap was made. A recap that was saved before November 24 doesn't include sessions until you regenerate it. Work that runs only on your computer isn't included, nor are cloud sessions where we can tell Claude used a tool on your computer, phone, or browser.

### Is my recap in my data export?

Your saved recaps are included, even those that the Reflect page doesn't show, for example with memory off, or from earlier months. Recaps that you deleted aren't. Nor is any other recap that the app hides, for example because a chat, session, or task that it used was deleted, or a newer recap for the same months replaced it. In some cases, a recap of a deleted chat, session, or task can stay stored for longer, and may appear in your data export until it's removed.

Data exports don't include the record of the time you have Claude open, the list of chats that a recap used, or your break reminder and quiet hours settings. You can export your data on the web or Claude Desktop.
