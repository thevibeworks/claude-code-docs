# Anthropic IPO: International Retail Brokers and Selling Agents

Anthropic’s proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock is expected to include retail offerings in Australia, Canada, certain member states of the European Economic Area (Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, Norway, Spain, and Sweden), Japan, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. Anthropic does not currently intend to list its Class A common stock on any exchange in such international jurisdictions.

The following brokers, financial intermediaries, and selling agents are expected to participate in the proposed retail offerings in the international jurisdictions indicated below. In these jurisdictions, Anthropic has not authorized any other firm or platform to offer, or to advertise access to, shares in the proposed retail offerings. This page will be updated if the list changes.

**Australia:** Bell Potter, CommSec, LGT Wealth, Macquarie, Morgans, Morgan Stanley Wealth Management, Sharesies, Stake.

**Canada:** BMO Capital Markets, CIBC Capital Markets, Morgan Stanley, RBC Capital Markets, Scotiabank, TD Securities, Wealthsimple.

**EEA and/or Switzerland:** BNP Paribas, Crédit Agricole CIB (and affiliates), Deutsche Bank, eToro, flatexDEGIRO, Interactive Brokers, Nordnet Bank AB, Revolut, Saxo Bank, Trade Republic, UBS Investment Bank.

**Japan:** Mitsubishi UFJ Morgan Stanley (and affiliates), Mizuho, Rakuten, SBI.

**UK:** AJ Bell, eToro, Hargreaves Lansdown, Interactive Brokers, Interactive Investor, Revolut. RetailBook is acting as the Public Offer Platform (POP) operator and is coordinating the retail offer for the UK.
