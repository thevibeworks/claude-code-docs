# Set an internal help contact for your organization

Available on Team and Enterprise plans, and configurable by Primary Owners and Owners.

An internal help contact is the person or team inside your organization that members contact about access, seats, and features your organization manages. When you set one, members see it in **[Settings > Account](https://claude.ai/settings/account)** in place of your Primary Owner's name and email.

**Note:** Setting a help contact doesn't change who your Primary Owner is, or anyone's role or permissions. It's also separate from support contacts, which control who can reach Anthropic's human support team on Enterprise plans. See **[Designate support contacts for human support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15263885-designate-support-contacts-for-human-support)**.

## Set a help contact

1. Navigate to **[Organization settings > Organization and access](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/organization)**.

2. Find the **Internal help contact** section.

3. Enter an address in **Email**. This can be a shared inbox, such as your IT help desk.

4. Optionally, enter the name of the person or team in **Name (optional)**, up to 100 characters.

5. Click "Save changes."

The contact must be an email address, not a link, and the address can't include accented or non-Latin characters. No confirmation email is sent, so check the address before you save.

To change the contact later, edit the fields and save again.

## What members see

Members see **Your organization's help contact** in **Settings > Account**, with the email address and the name, if you added one. If no contact is set, they see your Primary Owner's name and email.

- The Primary Owner doesn't see the contact on their own account page. To check how it looks, ask another member.

- Members of more than one organization see the contact for the organization they're currently using.

- The contact doesn't appear in the Claude mobile apps.

## Remove a help contact

1. Navigate to **[Organization settings > Organization and access](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/organization)**.

2. In the **Internal help contact** section, click "Remove contact."

Members see your Primary Owner's name and email again.
