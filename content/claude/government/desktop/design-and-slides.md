# Design and Slides in Claude Desktop

> Make a design or a slide deck in Claude Desktop for Claude for Government, find, export, or delete it, and turn Design and Slides on for your members.

> **Who this is for:** Anyone who uses Claude Desktop in Claude for Government. The last section is for administrators who turn Design and Slides on.

With Design and Slides, you ask Claude for a design or a slide deck. A design can be a mockup, a poster, or a web page, for example. The design or deck opens beside your conversation, and both you and Claude can edit it.

To edit or export a design or deck yourself, you need a mouse or another pointing device. With the keyboard alone, you can ask Claude to create and change one.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Design and Slides are in beta. They are off until [your administrators turn them on](#turn-on-design-and-slides), and they require Claude Desktop 6.6395.0 or later.

## Start a design or deck

Start from one of these places:

* **The Artifacts page**: click **Artifacts** in the sidebar. Under **Make something new**, click the **Design** or **Slides** card. A blank design or deck opens beside a message box, where you tell Claude what to make.
* **The home screen**: in the message box, type `/design` or `/slides`, then what you want, and send the message.
* **An open chat or task, or a project's page**: in Claude Desktop 7.37095.0 or later, type `/design` or `/slides` in the message box of that chat, task, or page, then what you want, and send the message.

You see the card and the command for each one your administrators turned on.

When Claude asks to create a design or deck, or to save a change to one, click **Create** or **Update**.

To give Claude your brand's colors and type to use, [add a design system on your computer](https://claude.com/docs/third-party/claude-desktop/design-and-slides.md#add-a-design-system-on-one-computer).

## Find a design or deck again

Click **Artifacts** in the sidebar, then click the design or deck to open it. Designs and decks are kept on your computer, so they are available only there. The zip file that moves your data to another computer [leaves them out](https://claude.com/docs/government/desktop/import.md#what-the-zip-file-leaves-out), so [export](#export-a-design-or-deck) the ones you want to keep first.

With Cowork, the **Artifacts** page also lists your other artifacts, the interactive pages that Claude builds for you.

## Export a design or deck

Open the design or deck, click its title, and select **Export**. You can export a design as a web page (HTML) or a PDF, and a deck as either of those or as a PowerPoint file.

## Delete a design or deck

Click **Artifacts** in the sidebar, open the menu on the design or deck, select **Delete**, and confirm.

When you delete the conversation where you made a design or deck, the title of the confirmation tells you what happens to it:

* **Delete chat**: the designs and decks made in the chat are deleted too.
* **Delete task**: the designs and decks made in the task stay on the **Artifacts** page.

## Turn on Design and Slides

1. As a tenant administrator or organization owner, go to **Settings > Config** in the admin portal.
2. Under **Product availability > Artifact types**, expand [**Design**](https://claude.com/docs/government/config/settings.md#design-in-claude-desktop) or [**Slides**](https://claude.com/docs/government/config/settings.md#slides-in-claude-desktop), turn on its switch, and click **Save changes**.
3. To turn on both, repeat step 2 for the other setting.

Members get the change when they [restart Claude Desktop](https://claude.com/docs/government/config/overview.md#when-changes-take-effect). To turn a switch on for some members only, see [Turn a feature on for one group](https://claude.com/docs/government/config/overview.md#turn-a-feature-on-for-one-group).
