# Account

> Check the email, organization, role, and seat tier on your Claude for Government account, who to ask to change each one, and what to do with no seat.

> **Who this is for:** Anyone with a Claude for Government account.

The **Account** tab shows who you are signed in as and what access you have been given. Your details on this page are read-only.

## What you'll see

The table shows what each field means and who to ask when it is wrong.

| Field | What it means | Who to ask |
| - | - | - |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with. Your name and email come from your agency's directory. | The team that runs your agency's identity provider or directory |
| **Organization** | The organization you belong to within your agency's [tenant](https://claude.com/docs/government/overview.md#how-your-deployment-is-organized). Users cannot move themselves between organizations. | Your organization's owner, who can pass the request to a tenant administrator |
| **Role** | What you are allowed to manage. **User** means you can use Claude but do not administer anything. **Owner** means you can manage your organization's users, seats, and settings. **Primary Owner** is the same as Owner with a few additional [safeguards](https://claude.com/docs/government/org-admin/users.md#change-a-role). | Your organization's owner |
| **Seat tier** | How much Claude usage you get (shown on the [Usage](https://claude.com/docs/government/account/usage.md) tab) and which Claude models you are allowed to use | Your organization's owner |

## If your seat tier says "No seat assigned"

Without a seat tier you can sign in and see the portal, but you cannot send any messages to Claude, and the [Usage](https://claude.com/docs/government/account/usage.md) tab will show "No seat tier assigned" instead of your allowance. Ask your organization's owner to assign you a seat.

> **For organization owners:** When you view your own profile with no seat, the page links you straight to **Admin → Users** so you can assign yourself one. Being an owner does not give you a seat by itself, because administrative access and Claude usage are granted separately. Seats can also be [assigned automatically](https://claude.com/docs/government/org-admin/seats.md#how-seats-are-assigned-automatically).
